Privacy Policy

At Mocare, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or contact our team. Please read this policy carefully. By using our website or services, you acknowledge the practices described below. If you have questions about how your information is handled, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

1. What this Privacy Policy covers

This policy applies to information collected through the Mocare website, appointment and contact forms, and communications with our team. It describes the types of information we may collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you. This policy does not apply to third-party websites or services that we do not operate; please review their privacy policies separately.

Information collected directly from you may include your name, contact details, appointment preferences, and any information you choose to provide when contacting us. Please do not submit sensitive health information through a general website form. When you browse our website, basic technical information such as browser type, device information, and pages visited may also be collected to help us maintain and improve the site.

2. How we collect and use your information

We collect information you provide when you request an appointment, fill out a form, contact our team, or otherwise communicate with us. Some technical information may be collected automatically through essential website technologies and server logs when you visit our pages.

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to your questions and manage appointment requests;
  • Provide, maintain, and improve our website and services;
  • Communicate with you about a request or an update to our services;
  • Protect the security of our website, users, and business; and
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help operate our website or respond to your requests, and only as needed for those purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or services.

3. Legal basis for processing personal data

Where applicable privacy laws require a legal basis for processing, we process personal information when it is necessary to respond to your request or provide a service, when you have given consent, to meet a legal obligation, or when it is necessary for our legitimate interests and those interests do not override your rights. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

4. How we protect and retain information

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use care when sending information online and avoid sending confidential medical details through general contact forms.

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide requested services, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or securely de-identify it.

5. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to receive a copy of information you provided to us. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding and will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect how parts of the website work.

6. Children's privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through the website, please contact us so that we can review and address the matter.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the date below and post the revised policy on this page. Please review this page periodically to stay informed.
